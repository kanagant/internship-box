# Where to look

Always open the posting's own page before listing it. A lead from a list is not proof it is open. Status: `seen` means the
URL appeared in a search result; `todo` means the team has not confirmed it.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SimplifyJobs, Summer 2027 internships (GitHub) | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships/dev/README.md | Public list kept up to date daily by a student club and Simplify. Categories: software engineering, product management, data science / AI / ML, quant finance, hardware. Repository names change each season; search for the current one. | seen |

## Source of truth

The company's own careers page or application link. Use it to confirm a posting is open, and to copy the deadline,
eligibility and requirements.
